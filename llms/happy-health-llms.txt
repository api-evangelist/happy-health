# Happy Health

> Happy Health, Inc. is an Austin, Texas digital health company behind the Happy Ring — an
> FDA-cleared, clinical-grade smart ring — and Happy Sleep, its at-home sleep-testing and
> sleep-apnea care service. The ring passively measures brain and body biomarkers and streams
> them to the Happy Sleep app for clinician and researcher review.

Generated by API Evangelist on 2026-08-22 from the Happy Health public surface. Happy Health
does not publish an llms.txt of its own; this file is a faithful summary of what is publicly
reachable, not a document authored by the company.

## Status of the API surface

Happy Health publishes **no public developer portal, API reference, SDK package or
machine-readable specification**. The company markets an integration surface to clinical-trial
sponsors, provider groups and researchers, but every route to it is a demo or sales
conversation. Verified 2026-08-22:

- `https://www.happysleep.com/tech` — "Cloud integration and SDK available" and "Follows SMART
  on FHIR interoperability standards so users can transmit, share, and download their health
  data." No reference, no spec, no SDK link.
- `https://happyring.fiftyseven.co/clinical-trials.html` — "easy integration into eClinical
  Platforms and EHR"; the only call to action is "Request a Demo".
- `docs.happysleep.com`, `developer.happysleep.com`, `portal.happysleep.com` — these are **not**
  developer sites. `*.happysleep.com` is a wildcard catch-all: every unknown subdomain 302s to
  `https://happysleep.com/select-billing-method`, the consumer checkout funnel.
- `api.happysleep.com` — an S3/CloudFront origin returning 403 AccessDenied on every path.
- `app.happysleep.com` — a Vercel-hosted single-page app currently showing "A better Happy
  Health is coming soon"; it answers 200 with the same HTML shell for every path.
- No `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/graphql`, `/llms.txt`,
  `/.well-known/agent-card.json` or `/.well-known/agent.json` on any host.
- `github.com/happy-health` is the company's real GitHub organisation (20 public repositories),
  but every one of them is a fork of a third-party library — Kronos, SQLite.swift, flatbuffers,
  swift-protobuf, memfault-firmware-sdk, GCDWebServer — used in the iOS app and ring firmware.
  There is no first-party client library, no CLI and no published spec.

## Product

- [Happy Sleep](https://www.happysleep.com/): at-home sleep test, virtual visit with a
  board-certified sleep doctor, diagnosis, prescription and personalised care plan; treatments
  include custom-fit oral devices, CPAP and surgery. Sold with insurance billing or self-pay.
- [Happy Ring](https://www.happysleep.com/tech): the wearable. Four LEDs, four electrodes, a
  three-axis accelerometer and two temperature sensors; ceramic exterior, medical-grade platinum
  skin sensors, IP67, up to three days of battery. Measures sleep apnea, sleep stages, total
  sleep time, awakenings, sleep quality, breathing quality, heart rate, temperature, blood
  oxygen, respiratory rate, heart-rate variability and activity.
- Clinical trials / decentralised trial use: remote onboarding, continuous streaming of up to 21
  patient biomarkers and endpoints, near real-time compliance monitoring.

## Compliance posture

Stated on the company's own pages: FDA 510(k) cleared (October 2024), built to be 21 CFR Part 11
compliant, follows HIPAA, NIST, COPPA, GDPR and CCPA guidelines, end-to-end passwordless
cryptographic security, and "Happy does not maintain or have access to a user's encryption keys."
No SOC 2, ISO 27001 or HITRUST certification is published, and there is no trust centre or
vulnerability-disclosure programme.
